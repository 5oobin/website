Soobin Cho

Hello! I'm a PhD candidate in Human Centered Design & Engineering (HCI) at the University of Washington, advised by Prof. David McDonald and Prof. Mark Zachry. I previously earned my MS in Engineering (HCI) from Seoul National University, and a BA in Communication & Media from Ewha Womans University.

soobin30[at]uw[dot]edu Curriculum Vitae Google Scholar LinkedIn

I am deeply interested in how computer systems help, or can help, us navigate our complex social needs when collaborating and communicating with others. My dissertation research focuses on how AI can support people in making sense of discussions in socially complex collaborative contexts such as Wikipedia.

Computer-Supported Cooperative Work · Computer-Mediated Communication · AI-Mediated Communication and Collaboration
